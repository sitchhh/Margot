## Observed features

The story opens with a familiar gym frustration, then introduces Magnolia and her
proposed gym. It mixes serious concerns about comfort and belonging with ordinary
details about childcare, lighting, workouts, and small treats. Gratitude is personal;
supporters are offered several ways to help. The risk section names upfront location
costs directly.

Two short source excerpts illustrate the humor:

- “because Utah” — a local aside in the soda-bar description.
- “zero glitter (because we respect your carpet)” — practical humor in a thank-you reward.

The rewards use comic exaggeration, conversational interruptions, and playful
comparisons. These are stylistic observations, not rules that every draft must copy.

## Editorial interpretation

Preserve specific observations, warmth, and comic timing in community copy. For
investor copy, retain the founder's directness while making evidence and the next
step easier to assess. Both profiles and their new examples are adaptations, not
quotations or Magnolia-approved final copy. Her corrections should guide refinement.

Keep current facts in [the project brief](../../../../docs/PROJECT.md) and approved
local profile, not here. A style reference does not refresh campaign claims.
