# Write in Pink Fitness Club's voice

Keep Magnolia's conviction, warmth, and attention to women's everyday experiences
across both tones. Follow her explicit direction for the piece and her latest edits.

## Choose the tone

- **Professional / investor:** Default for prospective and current investors,
  business partners, lenders, and formal introductions. Read
  [the professional profile](references/professional.md).
- **Casual / community:** Default for Kickstarter backers, community updates,
  newsletters, and social copy. Read [the casual profile](references/casual.md).

Choose from the message's purpose and actual relationship, not the recipient's
gender or a contact tag alone. A backer who is also an investor can receive either
tone. Follow an explicit tone request; for an existing exchange, preserve its
natural level of familiarity. Ask only if audience or purpose is genuinely unclear.
Read both profiles when asked for both versions.

## Ground the draft

Read [the project brief](../../../docs/PROJECT.md) for business facts and unresolved
questions. Use Maggie's confirmed current copy and supplied context for updates.
The Kickstarter informs style; its plans and reward dates do not establish today's
location, services, opening date, funding need, or investment terms. Do not turn
backer counts into members or investors, or historical pledges into current cash.

[Campaign voice notes](references/kickstarter-voice.md) record what was actually
read and which stylistic choices are interpretations. Consult them when calibrating
the voice or explaining its source. Professional wording is an adaptation for the
audience, not an existing investor voice approved by Magnolia.

For canonical introductions and unanswered follow-ups, apply this guidance to
approved profile fields or requested customization. Preserve template provenance;
do not silently rewrite approved templates or set `templates_approved` to true.
Examples are writing samples, not approved templates or sendable messages.

Produce the requested copy with any unresolved facts called out separately. One
version is enough unless alternatives were requested. Keep facts and commitments
the same when switching tones. Use the configured name and signature; ask if missing.

## Email approval

No email may be sent without Magnolia's (Maggie's) explicit approval of the final
message and recipient. This covers introductions, replies, follow-ups, community
updates, and test emails. Drafting, tone selection, template approval, and Rocky's
approval of repository changes do not authorize a send. Changes to approved copy
or recipients require her approval of the revised version; do not ask twice for an
unchanged message already explicitly approved for sending.

Any requested send must follow [the sending procedure](../../../docs/OUTREACH.md).
This skill only prepares copy. The current workflow supports one-recipient plain
text email; a community tone does not add bulk sending, scheduling, or publishing.
