# Map Maggie's network

Read [the outreach playbook](../../../docs/PLAYBOOK.md). Use this when Maggie asks
who she knows, where to start, or which connections might help Pink Fitness Club.
For public research on a particular prospect, use
[research a prospect](../margot-research-prospect/SKILL.md).

Start with what she has already told you. If her selected Supabase project is
available, read relevant contacts and notes so she doesn't have to repeat herself.
Otherwise continue in chat and be clear that records are unavailable.

Explore one useful circle at a time: past work, fitness, local business, advisers,
community, or people who offered help. Ask a few questions at a time, adapting to
her answers and available time rather than running a fixed-length interview.

For each person, capture what she knows:

- How and since when she knows them, in her words.
- Whether they might invest, introduce, advise, or some combination, and why.
- Whether a possible introduction is merely a hunch or has actually been offered.
- Known contact details, missing information, and a useful first step.

Do not infer wealth, investment eligibility, interest, or permission to pitch from
the relationship. Avoid collecting unrelated sensitive personal details. Looking
up a known person's address in Maggie's Gmail is an option within her request if
that account is connected; never guess an address or use someone else's mailbox.

Summarize the strongest supported paths and any questions still open. Let Maggie's
priorities and evidence guide the choice, without an arbitrary prospect score.

If she asks to save contacts, use [add a lead](../margot-add-lead/SKILL.md) and the
playbook's current CRM mapping. Preserve existing identities, statuses, and history;
append relationship/role context to notes. Missing required details remain explicit
unsaved drafts in chat. Don't write personal network maps into the repo.

Finish with what was saved versus only discussed, and one useful next step. Mapping
the network does not authorize contacting anyone or scheduling future work. A
separate explicit send instruction goes through the existing outreach procedure.
